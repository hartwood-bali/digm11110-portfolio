Escape to Hartwood Villa

Escape to Hartwood Villa Ubud, a private tropical sanctuary where modern elegance meets Bali’s natural beauty. Designed for comfort and relaxation, the villa features stylish interiors, a private swimming pool, a spacious terrace and thoughtful amenities to make every stay effortless.

Nestled in the heart of Ubud and moments from its renowned cultural attractions, Hartwood Villa offers the perfect balance of privacy, comfort and island charm creating an unforgettable retreat for couples, families and friends.

Your Stay

Thoughtfully designed for comfort and relaxation, Hartwood Villa offers two elegant bedrooms accommodating up to four guests. The villa features spacious living areas, a fully equipped kitchen, air-conditioned interiors and modern amenities throughout, creating the perfect setting for a memorable stay in Ubud.

Step outside to enjoy your private swimming pool and sun-soaked terrace, surrounded by lush tropical greenery. Whether you're sharing slow mornings over breakfast, exploring the cultural heart of Ubud or unwinding by the pool, every detail has been designed to help you relax and reconnect.

Local Attractions

Located a 12-minute walk from Neka Art Museum, the villa is also close to Blanco Museum (1.4 mi), Saraswati Temple (1.9 mi), and Monkey Forest Ubud (3.1 mi). Ngurah Rai International Airport is 22 mi away. Rafting opportunities are available in the surrounding area.
